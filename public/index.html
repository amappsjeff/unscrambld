How to play

One scrambled word a day. Put the letters back in order

  • Tap letters or type on your keyboard to build the word
  • You get 3 checks. If you’re close, you’ll see “Almost there!”
  • You get one hint: a random letter. It shows in your share result

More ways to play

  • Challenge: unscramble as many words as you can in 60 seconds
  • Archive: replay past days. These don’t count toward your streak
